🌿 Our Story

About Paws & Hearts

Dedicated to matching loving families with animals who need a second chance.

❤️ Our Mission

We Believe Every Pet
Deserves Love

Founded in 2016, Paws & Hearts started with a simple mission — to reduce the number of animals living without loving homes. What began as a small community effort in Mumbai has grown into a fully-fledged pet adoption center helping hundreds of animals every year.

We work tirelessly to rehabilitate, vaccinate, and prepare animals for adoption. More importantly, we take the time to match each pet with the perfect family, ensuring a lifelong bond that truly changes lives on both sides.

📋 How We Work

Our Adoption Process

We believe a great adoption starts with a great process — transparent, caring, and thorough.

1

Browse & Choose

Visit our pet gallery, read each pet's profile, and shortlist the ones that feel right for you.

2

Apply Online

Submit our adoption form with your household and lifestyle details so we can make the best match.

3

Review & Meet

Our team reviews your application and invites you for a meet-and-greet with your chosen pet.

4

Take Them Home

After approval, your new family member comes home — and our support doesn't stop there!
