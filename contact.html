❓ FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

📋

How long does adoption take?

After submitting your application, we typically respond within 2–3 business days. The full process, including meet-and-greet, usually takes 5–7 days.

💰

Is there an adoption fee?

Yes, there is a small adoption fee that covers vaccinations, microchipping, and health check-up costs. Fees vary by pet — contact us for details.

🏡

Can I foster instead of adopt?

Absolutely! Fostering is an amazing way to help animals in need temporarily. Contact us to learn about our fostering programme and how to get started.

🩺

Are pets vaccinated before adoption?

Yes! All our pets are fully vaccinated, dewormed, and examined by our vet before being put up for adoption. Their health certificates are provided.

🤝

Do you provide post-adoption support?

Yes! We provide ongoing support, guidance, and resources to all our adopters. You can reach out to our team any time after adoption.

🐾

Can I surrender a pet to you?

We do accept surrenders on a case-by-case basis, depending on current capacity. Please contact us first to discuss your situation.
